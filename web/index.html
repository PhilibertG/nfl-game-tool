Who gets open, and does the quarterback see it?

In American football the quarterback throws the ball to a teammate who runs a planned path. A pass is much easier to complete when that teammate is far from every defender, which is what “open” means. Using the position of all 22 players measured 10 times per second, we compute at every instant each receiver's chance of catching the ball if it were thrown to him right now.
